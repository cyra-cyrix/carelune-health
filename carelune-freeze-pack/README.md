## One-sentence decision

Carelune will be a **physiotherapist-led, coordinator-operated, multidisciplinary stroke-recovery continuity program**: licensed professionals retain discipline-specific clinical authority, while Carelune owns orchestration, caregiver execution, visibility, routing, auditability, and service closure.

## Freeze rule

During the first three-month pilot, add a feature only when it:

- addresses a safety, legal, privacy, or operational defect; or
- solves a recurring problem observed in at least two pilot patients or two participating professionals.

Clinical content and thresholds may be revised whenever the clinical governance group identifies a safety or evidence issue.
