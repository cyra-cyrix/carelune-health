# Global Benchmark and Borrow/Avoid Map

## Executive conclusion

No single company is the complete Carelune model. The best reference architecture is:

- **Kandu Health** for navigator-led, whole-person post-stroke transition;
- **Constant Therapy** for clinician-governed digital content, home practice, and granular performance data;
- **autonom-e** for configurable pathways and validated patient-reported outcome measures;
- **BTS Telerehab** for multidisciplinary, synchronous/asynchronous rehabilitation plans and formal documentation;
- **Sword Health** for clinician ownership, engaging guided sessions, and payer-aligned managed care—but not for copying an MSK sensor model into neuro-rehabilitation;
- **MindMaze** for high-dose, engaging neuro-rehabilitation and regulated digital assessment/device thinking—but not for the pilot’s cost or scope;
- **Motus Nova** for increasing repetitions at home—but only as a future device partner pattern.

Carelune’s differentiated combination is: **trusted existing therapist + named coordinator + one multidisciplinary plan + caregiver execution + exception routing + professional review + Indian home-context adaptation**.

## Primary reference companies

### 1. Kandu Health — closest service-model analogue

Kandu supports stroke survivors and care partners after discharge through licensed navigators, a stroke-specific app, goals, education, care coordination, mental-health connections, social-needs support, and provider visibility. Its care team spans multiple disciplines. [Kandu overview](https://kanduhealth.com/), [Kandu services](https://kanduhealth.com/our-services/)

**Borrow**

- one named recovery navigator/coordinator;
- patient and care-partner support, not patient-only design;
- goals and barriers, including mental health and social determinants;
- referral to external specialists rather than employing everyone immediately;
- 30/90-day follow-up and collection of a recognised disability outcome such as mRS;
- peer support as a later retention feature.

**Adapt for Carelune**

- acquisition begins with the existing neuro-physiotherapist, not hospitals;
- the Lead Physiotherapist owns the rehabilitation plan;
- the coordinator owns workflow, not independent clinical decisions;
- use an Indian family-pays pilot before pursuing hospital or payer reimbursement.

**Avoid**

- using preliminary, uncontrolled company outcomes as proof of causality;
- expanding immediately into a large multidisciplinary employed workforce;
- assuming US Principal Illness Navigation reimbursement transfers to India.

### 2. Constant Therapy — best content/data-loop analogue

Constant Therapy lets clinicians assign personalised speech, language, and cognitive home programmes and see detailed progress. It separates clinician tooling from patient practice and states that it supplies tools rather than guaranteed rehabilitation outcomes. It also supports Indian English. [Clinician product](https://constanttherapyhealth.com/constant-therapy/for-clinicians-adult-speech-therapy/), [technology](https://constanttherapyhealth.com/science/technology/), [company/product boundary](https://constanttherapyhealth.com/)

**Borrow**

- clinician-governed, metadata-rich intervention library;
- content assigned to goals and impairment domains;
- adaptive difficulty only within approved bounds;
- granular attempt, accuracy, assistance, and dosage data;
- patient/clinician ability to accept or adjust suggested programmes;
- Indian-language and culturally adapted content strategy;
- clear disclaimer separating tools from clinical services and guarantees.

**Adapt**

- include physiotherapy, OT, communication, swallow/feeding education, caregiver handling, nutrition, and psychological support as separately governed domains;
- AI may rank approved content, but the relevant professional must approve assignment and parameters.

**Avoid**

- treating app-task improvement as equivalent to functional recovery;
- automated progression for high-risk mobility or swallowing tasks;
- one generic content library with permissions shared across disciplines.

### 3. autonom-e — best configurable pathway and PROM analogue

autonom-e provides remote assessment, triage, monitoring, rehabilitation, group therapy, configurable follow-up, and more than 70 validated PROMs for health providers and research teams. [autonom-e platform](https://autonom-e.com/)

**Borrow**

- configurable pathways rather than diagnosis-specific hard-coding;
- validated measures stored as distinct instruments;
- scheduled measurement, reminders, and longitudinal charts;
- cohort management and discharge/patient-initiated follow-up;
- ability to add local questionnaires without labelling them validated.

**Avoid**

- placing dozens of assessments in the stroke MVP;
- turning caregiver input into an assessment burden;
- mixing local operational questionnaires with validated PROM scores.

### 4. BTS Telerehab — best multidisciplinary workflow analogue

BTS supports individual rehabilitation plans, editable scores/questionnaires, teleconsultation, synchronous and asynchronous motor, cognitive and speech therapy, devices, records, reports, and multidisciplinary pathways. [BTS Telerehab](https://www.btsbioengineering.com/products/bts-telerehab/)

**Borrow**

- one individual rehabilitation plan with discipline-specific ownership;
- synchronous, caregiver-guided, and asynchronous video-review modes;
- formal session documentation and plan-version history;
- third-party-device readiness as a future integration principle;
- final reports assembled from structured activity data.

**Avoid**

- purchasing/building its device-heavy ecosystem in the pilot;
- claiming constant remote supervision;
- letting technical capability determine what is clinically collected.

### 5. Sword Health — best managed digital-therapy operating benchmark

Sword pairs a dedicated physical therapist with personalised programmes, guided home sessions, movement feedback, education, and payer/employer distribution. [Sword virtual care model](https://swordhealth.com/articles/virtual-pt-what-works), [member programme description](https://meet.swordhealth.com/thrive/humana-member)

**Borrow**

- named professional ownership;
- personalised content rather than generic videos;
- engagement and correct execution as first-class product problems;
- education and behavioural support alongside exercises;
- outcomes-linked commercial thinking once evidence is mature.

**Avoid**

- importing claims from MSK populations into stroke;
- sensors/computer vision before the core service works;
- 24/7 access promises and hardware logistics;
- payer-first go-to-market in the Bengaluru pilot.

### 6. MindMaze — future regulated neurotechnology benchmark

MindMaze offers clinic/home neuro-rehabilitation, interactive therapy, movement/cognitive assessments, peripherals, and regulated products. [MindMaze platform](https://mindmaze.com/), [MindMotion GO](https://mindmaze.com/mindmotion-go/), [healthcare portfolio](https://mindmaze.com/about/healthcare/)

**Borrow later**

- engaging, high-repetition task design;
- clear separation of therapy, care, and measurement products;
- validation and regulatory strategy for objective digital assessments;
- partnership rather than reinvention when devices become useful.

**Avoid now**

- immersive hardware, proprietary peripherals, or medical-device claims;
- treating activity telemetry as validated functional assessment;
- capital-intensive deployment before retention and unit economics are proven.

### 7. Motus Nova — future home-dose/device benchmark

Motus Nova uses home robotic devices to increase upper- and lower-limb repetitions and provides reports and support. [Motus Nova products](https://motusnova.com/products/)

**Borrow later**

- rehabilitation dose and repetition as meaningful process data;
- device rental/subscription or partner integrations;
- independence from constant caregiver assistance where clinically appropriate.

**Avoid now**

- owning hardware inventory;
- making device engagement the main business;
- relying on self-reported company outcomes as Carelune evidence.

## Secondary references

- **HeadApp:** professional cognitive/language library, remote assignments, and progress measurement. Borrow structured domain-specific content; avoid broadening the pilot into a cognitive-training product. [HeadApp](https://headapp.us/)
- **PRISM:** dedicated nurse/care partner and post-stroke goals/coordination. Borrow reassurance and named-person experience; avoid universal daily biometrics and continuous-monitoring implications. [PRISM](https://www.myprism.ai/)
- **OrthoRPM / SCRENR:** clinician dashboard, adherence capture, motion data, documentation and practice revenue. Borrow exception-led workflow and low-friction activation; avoid US billing-code assumptions and unvalidated stroke motion claims. [OrthoRPM](https://www.ortho-rpm.com/clinician), [SCRENR](https://www.screnrhealth.com/)

## Business-model recommendation

### Pilot

- family pays for a managed 30-day programme;
- referring physiotherapist receives transparent professional compensation and retains direct-visit revenue;
- specialist consultations/direct visits are separate or explicitly bundled;
- Carelune initially earns from programme orchestration, not marketplace commissions.

### After evidence

1. clinic-branded programme with per-active-patient fees;
2. hospital discharge partnership with implementation + patient fee/revenue share;
3. employer/insurer outcomes contract only after credible comparative evidence;
4. licensed content/pathway infrastructure only after the managed service reveals repeatable workflows.

## Non-copyable moat to build

- physiotherapist trust and repeat referral behaviour;
- coordinator playbook and service-level closure;
- governed multidisciplinary content and protocol versions;
- longitudinal relationship between goals, prescribed intervention, actual execution, barriers, professional decisions, and outcomes;
- Indian caregiver, language, home-environment, and affordability adaptations;
- evidence-quality labelling and auditability.
