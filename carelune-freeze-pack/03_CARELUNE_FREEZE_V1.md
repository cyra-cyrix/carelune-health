# Carelune Product Freeze V1

## Brand and offer

**Carelune Health — Care continues.**

First programme: **Carelune Neuro Continuum — Recovery beyond discharge.**

Family promise: **Bring the patient home without losing the recovery team.**

Working pilot offer: a paid 30-day programme, initially **₹5,999/month**, with final inclusions and professional fees confirmed before sale.

## Product category

Carelune is a **multidisciplinary recovery orchestration platform delivered as a managed continuity programme**. It is not an exercise-video app, marketplace, virtual hospital, emergency service, or autonomous clinical decision system.

## Pilot model

- Bengaluru cluster;
- three medically stable adult stroke patients first;
- expand to 10–15 only after workflow review;
- physiotherapist-led referral;
- referring physiotherapist remains Lead Physiotherapist;
- family pays Carelune;
- physical visits are paid directly to the visiting professional and initially carry no Carelune commission;
- Medical Clinician/PM&R/other specialist consultations are separately billed unless clearly included;
- one named Recovery Care Coordinator is the operational front face.

## Roles

### Product and business

Sujith owns technology, UX, workflow automation, pricing, partnerships, analytics, finance, and scaling.

### Clinical operations

Dr. Farhan is displayed as **Medical Clinician & Clinical Operations Lead**. These are conceptually separate permissions even if one person holds both roles.

### Clinical ownership

- Lead Physiotherapist: mobility and physiotherapy plan;
- Occupational Therapist: ADLs, upper-limb function, cognition/task adaptation, home environment;
- Speech and Swallow Therapist: communication and swallowing/feeding interventions;
- Rehabilitation Nurse: structured nursing triage and education within approved pathways;
- Medical Clinician: medical assessment, medical advice, and medication decisions after a documented consultation;
- PM&R Specialist: complex rehabilitation strategy, protocol governance, and multidisciplinary review;
- Dietitian: individual nutrition assessment and plan;
- Clinical Psychologist/qualified mental-health professional: patient adjustment, mood, behaviour, and caregiver/family support;
- Coordinator: onboarding, scheduling, engagement, routing, SLA tracking, and closure—no diagnosis or treatment decisions.

## Plans

### Core programme

- coordinator onboarding and caregiver training;
- Lead Physiotherapist-approved patient plan;
- caregiver daily priorities and task reporting;
- weekly Lead Physiotherapist review;
- defined-hours coordinator support and nursing triage;
- progress summary, action timeline, monthly review;
- coordination of specialist review when indicated.

### Premium/add-on services

- OT, speech/swallow, dietitian, psychology, or additional PM&R review;
- multidisciplinary case conference;
- direct home/clinic visits;
- higher-touch caregiver/family support.

Clinical eligibility is independent of package price. A premium payment cannot make an unsafe patient suitable for the service.

## Product pillars

1. functional goals above tasks;
2. governed multidisciplinary content library;
3. one patient-specific integrated plan with discipline ownership;
4. “today’s priorities” for the caregiver;
5. six execution outcomes: completed, partial, unable, refused, unwell, need help;
6. patient-specific daily check-in, not universal biometrics;
7. unified patient/action timeline;
8. exception routing with owner, response target, follow-up, and closure;
9. generic review and specialist-referral engines;
10. plan versions, clinical rationale, and audit events;
11. goals, barriers, home environment, equipment, and caregiver capability;
12. progress presented without unsupported outcome claims.

## AI boundary

Allowed: approved-content discovery/ranking, summaries, draft notes, metadata, translation/captions, missing-data prompts, and potential contraindication flags.

Forbidden without human approval: diagnosis, prescribing, assigning treatment, changing medication, progressing/regressing/holding therapy, declaring swallowing safe, deciding that direct care is unnecessary, or closing a clinical escalation.

## Support boundary

- Coordinator: 8:00 AM–8:00 PM IST, seven days;
- Nursing triage: 8:00 AM–8:00 PM IST, seven days;
- Medical Clinician: scheduled consultations and same-day escalation where priority and availability permit;
- no guaranteed immediate or 24/7 clinician access.

Emergency copy:

> Carelune is not an emergency service. For severe breathing difficulty, unconsciousness, a new stroke-like symptom, seizure lasting more than five minutes, serious fall, or another emergency, call 112/108 or go to the nearest emergency department immediately.

## Three-month proof points

Primary business evidence:

- eligible referral → paid conversion;
- month-two and month-three continuation/appropriate step-down;
- repeat physiotherapist referrals;
- coordinator time per active patient;
- direct contribution margin;
- critical workflow/SLA completion;
- caregiver and professional experience;
- safety events, readmissions, and documentation defects.

Do not claim efficacy from a small uncontrolled pilot. Report feasibility, engagement, safety, service performance, and descriptive clinical change.
